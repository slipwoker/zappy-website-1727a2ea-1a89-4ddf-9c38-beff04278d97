Expert Roofing Replacement & Repair

From weather-worn shingles to complete roof replacements, our team delivers durable, watertight results that protect your home for decades. We use premium materials and proven installation techniques to ensure every layer—from underlayment to ridge cap—is flawless.

  • Comprehensive inspections identify hidden leaks, rot, and structural weaknesses before they become costly emergencies.
  • Full tear-off and replacement with architectural shingles, metal roofing, or flat-roof systems tailored to New England weather.
  • Photo updates throughout the project so you see exactly what's happening above your head.
We commonly solve: persistent ceiling stains, missing shingles, ice dam damage, and poor attic ventilation causing premature roof failure.
02

Interior & Exterior House Painting

Our painting process goes far beyond a simple coat of color. We prioritize meticulous surface preparation—power washing, scraping, sanding, caulking, and priming—so the finish is smooth, even, and built to withstand sun, rain, and seasonal shifts.

  • Thorough prep work eliminates peeling paint at the source, not just covering it up temporarily.
  • Premium paints and stains applied with brush, roller, and spray techniques for consistent coverage on siding, trim, and doors.
  • Clean, contained job sites with daily cleanup so your family's routine stays uninterrupted.
We commonly solve: blistering and peeling exterior paint, faded siding, moisture intrusion behind failing caulk, and dated interior color schemes.
03

Gutter Installation & Guard Systems

Properly sized and pitched gutters are your home's first defense against foundation damage, basement flooding, and landscape erosion. We install seamless aluminum gutters and effective guard systems that keep water flowing and debris out.

  • Seamless gutter runs custom-fabricated on-site for a perfect fit with no leaky joints.
  • Gutter guards installed to prevent clogs from leaves and pine needles, reducing ladder trips.
  • Downspout extensions and drainage solutions that direct water safely away from your foundation.
We commonly solve: overflowing gutters, sagging sections pulling away from fascia, basement water intrusion, and ice buildup in winter.
04

Chimney Flashing & Leak Prevention

Chimney leaks are among the most deceptive home issues—water travels far from the entry point before showing stains. We specialize in copper and aluminum flashing, cricket installation, and crown repair to stop leaks at the source.

  • Old flashing removed and replaced with step, counter, and apron flashing for a watertight seal.
  • Cricket (saddle) installation behind wide chimneys to divert water and prevent debris buildup.
  • Chimney crown repair and waterproofing to protect masonry from freeze-thaw cycles.
We commonly solve: water stains around the fireplace, musty attic odors near the chimney chase, deteriorated mortar crowns, and rusted flashing.
